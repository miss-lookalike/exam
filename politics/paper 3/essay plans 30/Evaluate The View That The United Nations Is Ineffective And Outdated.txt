Introduction
Key Definitions
The United Nations UN was created after the end of WWII, with the aim of promoting peace
and achieving international cooperation between nation to solve international problems of an
economic, social and humanitarian character.
This essay will analyse whether it is now ineffective and outdated by looking at three of its key
institutions: the Security Council, General Assembly and International Court of Justice.
Paragraph One ➡ United Nations Security Council (UNSC)
Against: The United Nations Is Effective and Isnʼt Outdated
The UN Security Council is the most powerful organ of the United Nations, due to its ability to
make legally binding decisions, such as the imposition of sanctions, authorisation of the use of
force, and the establishment of peacekeeping missions.Evaluate The View That The United Nations Is Ineffective And Outdated. 30 Politics Explained Essay Plan1
It is able to use these powers to maintain international peace, promote collective security
and enforce international law.
One central argument that the UNSC isnʼt outdated and continues to play an effective role in
maintaining peace and enforcing international law is its ability to convene quickly and respond
to global crises. Unlike other organs of the UN, it is able to meet quickly and this is crucial when
handling conflicts, humanitarian emergencies, or situations that require immediate international
action.
In 2006, for example, the UNSC responded quickly to news of North Korean nuclear tests
by imposing sanctions designed to pressure the regime to halt its nuclear program. These
sanctions targeted North Koreaʼs access to weapons, luxury goods, and financial resources
critical to its nuclear activities.
Crucially, the UNSC also has the power to authorise actions to resolve conflicts and compel
states rather than relying on statesʼ compliance. This includes through imposing sanctions and
deploying peacekeeping forces.
In the early 2000s, Liberia was in the throes of a brutal civil war, with widespread human
rights abuses and massive displacement of civilians. In response in 2003, the UNSC passed
Resolution 1509, establishing the United Nations Mission in Liberia UNMIL, a
peacekeeping operation tasked with stabilising the country.
The mission was successful in disarming over 100,000 combatants, supporting the
peaceful transition to a democratic government, and facilitating the reintegration of
displaced persons.
UNMIL was widely praised for its role in helping Liberia transition from conflict to peace,
demonstrating the Security Councilʼs capacity to mobilise resources quickly and
respond effectively to humanitarian crises and conflicts.
Importantly, the UNSCʼs decisions and actions also carry significant international legitimacy, as
they are often seen as representing the collective will of the global community. This legitimacy
comes from the fact that the Council includes both permanent members (representing the
worldʼs major powers) and non-permanent members (reflecting broader regional
representation).
Its decisions, particularly when they are supported by a broad consensus, are considered
binding on all UN member states, making them more powerful than decisions from other
international institutions, therefore strengthening the actions taken by the UNSC.
Importantly, the veto power held by five of the worldʼs most important powers ensures that
no major power is forced into an action against their will, therefore preventing the escalation
and threat of nuclear war that could occur as a result.
This also prevents some states from enforcing one-sided decisions which arenʼt
supported by the international community and could spark conflict.
For example, in 1957, the UNSC discussed the ongoing dispute between India and
Pakistan over the region of Kashmir.
The US and UK supported a resolution that favoured Pakistanʼs position, calling for
the demilitarisation of Kashmir and a plebiscite to determine its future. However, the
Soviet Union used its veto power to block the resolution, arguing that it unfairly
favoured one party and could escalate tensions between the two countries.Evaluate The View That The United Nations Is Ineffective And Outdated. 30 Politics Explained Essay Plan2
The Soviet veto prevented what could have been a one-sided resolution from being
enforced, which might have deepened the conflict. This shows how the veto can be
an effective tool for balance and preventing escalation.
For: The United Nations Is Ineffective and Outdated
On the other hand, it can be argued that the the UN Security Council UNSC is outdated and
ineffective, because the permanent seat given to the P5 members (the UK, US, France, China
and Russia) and the veto they have gives them an unfair level of power and often results in
gridlock and the inability to prevent conflict.
The composition of the UNSC reflects an outdated post-WWII order and makes it ineffective in
responding to crises in a consistent manner.
The P5 structure doesnʼt share power equally or adequately represent the contemporary
distribution of power and influence in global politics. This lack of broader representation
diminishes the legitimacy of the Security Council and its decisions in the eyes of many
member states, particularly those from the Global South, who feel their voices are
marginalised in critical international decision-making processes.
Rising powers like India, Brazil, and Japan do not hold permanent seats, despite their
growing economic and political influence.
Similarly, Africa, which contains a large number of UN member states, has no
permanent representation in the Security Council.
The legitimacy of the UNSC is further eroded by the fact that the P5 members make
decisions and use their veto based on their advancing their national agendas, rather than
focusing on global peace and security. This creates inconsistency, bias and can result in
prolonged conflicts, human suffering, and missed opportunities for effective conflict
resolution.
This also reflects the realist perspective that IGOs have little power and states are
simply motivated by pursuing their own interests.
During Israelʼs war in Gaza since October 2023, Israelʼs military campaign has killed
over 50,000 Palestinians the majority of which have been civilians and there have been
been numerous allegations of war crimes from international human rights groups such
as Amnesty International.
Despite this, the UNSC has failed to pass any binding resolutions to halt the violence
or hold parties accountable, due to repeated vetoes by the United States, which is a
close ally and strong military supporter of Israel.
This is a clear example of the failure of the UNSC to prevent conflict and uphold
human rights in relation to certain countries who have powerful allies, due to the
veto power that P5 members have.
Similar occurred when Russiaʼs veto prevented UNSC action to address the Russia-
Ukraine war and Russiaʼs human rights violations, as well as Bashar Al Assadʼs human
rights violations for a decade in the Syrian Civil War
Finally, the UNSC can also be argued to be ineffective as many of its resolutions and sanctions
are ineffective in achieving their intended goals. While the Council can pass binding resolutionsEvaluate The View That The United Nations Is Ineffective And Outdated. 30 Politics Explained Essay Plan3
and impose sanctions, enforcement is frequently weak, and compliance from member states is
inconsistent.
Sanctions are often used as a tool to coerce states into compliance with international law or
human rights norms. However, in many cases, sanctions fail to change the behaviour of
targeted governments.
Countries like North Korea, Iran, and Venezuela have endured long-term sanctions with
limited impact on their leadership or policies, while the general population often suffers the
most from the economic restrictions.
Judgement
Overall, it is clear that the structure of the UNSC, in particular the veto given to P5 members, is
outdated and prevents it from upholding international peace and security.
Not only is the structure unrepresentative of the current global order and gives far too much
power to the permanent members, these members use the their vetoes to further their
geopolitical interests and this often results in the prolonging of conflicts.
Paragraph Two ➡ The United Nations General Assembly (UNGA)
Against: The United Nations Is Effective and Isnʼt Outdated
The UN General Assembly UNGA is the main deliberative body of the United Nations, where
all 193 member states are represented equally, each with one vote. The UN General Assemblyʼs
primary purpose is to promote international cooperation on issues related to peace, security,
human rights, economic development, and social progress.
The UNGA provides as an important forum for diplomatic engagement and allows the
international community as a whole to apply moral pressure on states.
The annual General Debate allows leaders from all member states to present their views on
international challenges. This helps ensure that the concerns of all nations, not just the
powerful, are heard.
For example, Cuban leader Fidel Castro famously used the 1960 General Debate to
criticise U.S. foreign policy, demonstrating how the platform allows smaller or isolated
nations to voice their perspectives on the global stage.
More recently, the UNGAʼs emergency special session in March 2022 condemned Russiaʼs
invasion of Ukraine, with 141 countries voting in favour of a resolution demanding an
immediate withdrawal.
While the resolution was non-binding, it demonstrated broad international opposition to
the invasion, isolating Russia politically and raising its diplomatic costs.
The most significant way in which the UNGA can be argued to be effective is through setting
moral and political standards that influence the behaviour of states and the international
community. Its resolutions may be non binding, but it can still influence state behaviour.
For instance, the Universal Declaration of Human Rights 1948, though not legally
enforceable, laid the groundwork for modern human rights law and has influenced
numerous international treaties and national constitutions, including the European
Convention on Human Rights.Evaluate The View That The United Nations Is Ineffective And Outdated. 30 Politics Explained Essay Plan4
Similarly, the UNGA has been instrumental in advancing sustainable development through
its adoption of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and the Sustainable
Development Goals SDGs).
While the goals are non-binding, they have galvanised global cooperation on issues
such as poverty, inequality, and climate change, providing a shared framework for
action by governments, civil society, and the private sector.
Liberals would argue that this shows the power of IGOs to influence state behaviour
and bring states together to tackle common goals.
The UNGA also devoted a significant portion of the UN budget towards achieving these
goals.
For: The United Nations Is Ineffective and Outdated
On the other hand, it can be argued that the UNGA is ineffective as it has little real power and
therefore isnʼt able to do much, despite being the organ of the United Nations that is the most
representative of the international community.
The main criticisms of the General Assembly is that its resolutions are non-binding. Unlike
the Security Council, which can pass legally enforceable resolutions, the General
Assemblyʼs decisions do not carry the force of law, meaning that member states are not
obligated to comply.
This has led many to view the Assembly as a forum for debate rather than action, with
little power to make a tangible impact on international conflicts or crises.
For example, Resolution 73/98 was passed in 2018 ruling that Israeli settlements in the occupied
Palestinian territories, including East Jerusalem and the West Bank, are illegal under
international law. This had no impact on the ground, however, as Israel wasnʼt required to
comply and the US has consistently vetoed any binding measures against Israel in the UNSC.
Another example is the General Assemblyʼs numerous resolutions against nuclear weapons,
such as calling for global nuclear disarmament, which have not compelled nuclear powers
to dismantle their arsenals or sign binding disarmament agreements.
These examples highlight how the General Assemblyʼs resolutions often serve as
symbolic gestures rather than tools for enforcing international law or resolving conflicts.
This also further demonstrates the realist view that states ignore IGOs and simply
pursue their national interests.
Judgement
Overall, the more persuasive argument is that the UNGA is ineffective and outdated. As the
most representative organ of the UN, it should be the body with the most power however in
reality the structure of the UN means it has little influence.
Though it can have some influence in setting moral standards and passing resolutions, it
ultimately relies on states to enforce these measure which they often donʼt.
Paragraph Three ➡ The International Court of Justice (ICJ)
Against: The United Nations Is Effective and Isnʼt OutdatedEvaluate The View That The United Nations Is Ineffective And Outdated. 30 Politics Explained Essay Plan5
The International Court of Justice ICJ, also known as the World Court, is the principal judicial
organ of the United Nations, with the purpose of settling legal disputes between states and
providing advisory opinions on international legal matters referred to it by the UN.
This is intended to promote the peaceful resolution of international disputes and contribute
to the development of international law.
By providing a legal forum where states can resolve conflicts without resorting to war or
violence, the ICJ strengthens the rule of law at the global level and enhances international
cooperation on issues such as territorial disputes, maritime boundaries, and diplomatic
relations. Its rulings carry significant moral and political weight, which creates pressure on
states to comply.
For example, in a territorial dispute between Costa Rica and Nicaragua over the San Juan
River, the ICJ ruled in favour of Costa Rica in 2018, requiring Nicaragua to compensate for
environmental damage. Both countries complied with the ruling, highlighting the Courtʼs role
in resolving environmental and territorial issues.
Another example is the 2022 resolution of a long-standing case regarding Ugandaʼs military
intervention in the Democratic Republic of Congo during the late 1990s.
The ICJ ordered Uganda to pay $325 million in reparations to the DRC for damages
caused by violations of international law, including human rights abuses and the illegal
exploitation of natural resources. Uganda complied with the judgement and the first
payment has already been made.
For: The United Nations Is Ineffective and Outdated
On the other hand, it can be argued that the ICJ is ineffective and outdated the vast majority of
the time, as states must consent to its jurisdiction for disputes to be heard and for rulings to be
enforced. If a state refuses to recognise the Courtʼs authority or declines to implement its
rulings, there are few mechanisms to compel compliance.
This is particularly true of powerful states, which can simply ignore rulings in the knowledge
that other states are unlikely to try and punish them for doing so through sanctions.
This can be seen in the case between China and the Philippines in 2016. In this maritime
dispute over the South China Sea, the Court ruled in favour of the Philippines, based on
international law under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea UNCLOS.
China, however, refused to recognise the Courtʼs ruling, citing its rejection of the
tribunalʼs authority, demonstrating how states can simply choose not to comply with
unfavourable decisions.
Similarly, in 2024 in the case of South Africa v. Israel the ICJ issued a ruling ordering
Israel to prevent genocidal acts and let humanitarian aid into Gaza, whilst late ordering
Israel to immediately halt its military offensive in Rafah in May 2024.
Israel didnʼt immediately halt its offensive in Rafah and has stopped humanitarian aid
from entering Gaza on numerous occasions since the ruling.
This demonstrates how the ICJ is ineffective due to its limited enforcement power
and reliance on state compliance for enforcement, as the UN doesnʼt have its own
police force.Evaluate The View That The United Nations Is Ineffective And Outdated. 30 Politics Explained Essay Plan6
Additionally, advisory opinions issued by the ICJ are non-binding, meaning that states can
ignore them without facing legal consequences. This undermines the Courtʼs ability to act as a
decisive force in resolving international conflicts or holding states accountable for violations of
international law.
For example, on response to Ukraineʼs claim of genocide, the ICJ issued an advisory
opinion that ordered Russia to immediately halt its military operations in Ukraine. Despite the
ruling, Russia refused to comply, continuing its invasion, which highlighted the Courtʼs
inability to enforce its decisions when powerful states choose to ignore them.
Judgement
Overall, this once more shows that the UN is outdated an ineffective as its judicial arm has no
real power to enforce its decisions, which allows powerful states to simply ignore its decisions,
therefore reinforcing the unequal international order.
Overall Argument
In conclusion, it is clear that the United Nations is ineffective and outdated. When it was
founded, significant power was given to the Security Council and in particular the P5 members
in it, whilst the other organs ere given little power to compel states.
This means that today the most representative organ and one of the most important organs with
the role of settling disputes to further peace; the UNGA and the ICJ respectively, have little real
power to influence global politics, uphold peace or enforce international law. As a result, they
are both ineffective in doing so.
By contrast, the P5 members of the UNSC have significant power to uphold peace through the
Security Councilʼs ability to issue enforcement measures. However, the veto means that the
UNSC more often prolongs conflicts rather than upholding peace, as the P5 members use their
vetoes to further their geopolitical interests.
This outdated structure maintains power in Russia, China, the US, the UK and France, failing
to represent the whole of the world or reflect changes to the global order.
