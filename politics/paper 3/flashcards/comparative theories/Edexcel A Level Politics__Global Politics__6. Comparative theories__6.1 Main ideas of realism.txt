Why does international anarchy lead towards tension and conflict according to neorealists?	<ul><li>States are self reliant and must look out for their own interests. 'Self help' forces states to ensure security and surival by building up military capacity to deter states from attacking them.&nbsp;</li><li>This is liable to be interpreted by other states as aggressive, creating a security dilemma (Booth and Wheeler, 2008)</li><li>Uncertainty and sustpicion are inherrent as no state can know the intensions of another state</li><li>There is no higher authority or leviathan that can impose international order. John Mearsheimer said 'there is no night watchman' Therefore leads to conflict.&nbsp;</li></ul>
What is the difference betwen offensive and defensive realists?	<ul><li>Defensive realists, such as Kenneth Waltz, emphasise that nation states are most influenced by the need for security. Nation states are therefore 'security maximisers'.&nbsp;</li><li>Offensive realists, such as John Mearsheimer, argue that nation states are more predatory, and as power maximisers, seek influence as well as security&nbsp;</li></ul>
Why do <b>neo</b>realists believe a believe a bipolar system tends towards strengh and stability?	<ul><li>Few great powers means the possibilities of great power wars are reduced.&nbsp;</li><li>The existance of only two great powers reduces the chance of miscalculation and makes it easier to operate an effective system of deterrence.&nbsp;</li></ul>
What is the connection between realism and (core) conservative philosophy?	<ul><li>Realism is based on the view that nation states act out of self interests and so are power maximisers.&nbsp;</li><li>Nation states must protect themselves from the predatory instincts of others through military and diplomatic means.&nbsp;</li><li>Hobbes feared the warlike instincts of human nature so prioritise security over all other considerations.&nbsp;</li><li>Conservative distrust utopianism (Oakeshott) - which is why realists emphasise national security rather than reliance on the UN.&nbsp;</li></ul>
