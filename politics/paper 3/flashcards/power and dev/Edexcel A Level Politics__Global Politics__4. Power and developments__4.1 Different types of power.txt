What is hard power?&nbsp;	<ul><li>Hard power involves inducements applied to a target state in order to secure a desired policy outcome.&nbsp;</li><li>Viewed as a 'zero sum' expression of power.&nbsp;</li><li><b>Instruments include&nbsp;</b></li><ul><li><b>Threat of force</b> - Kim Jong Un's threat to launch inter-continental ballistic missiles in America. Putin's threats to Ukraine. (Tend to have ultimatums attached)&nbsp;</li><li><b>Targeted military strikes</b> - US assasination of Iranian General Qassem Soleimani. Bombing in Libya.&nbsp;</li><li><b>Annexation</b> of a sovereign state's territory - Russia's annexation of Crimea in 2014.&nbsp;</li><li><b>Military exercises</b> can be seen as preperation for the use of force - e.g. NATO military exercises in the Baltics.&nbsp;</li><li><b>Economic sanctions</b> - Sanctions placed on Iran, Russia's removal from the SWIFT payment system, embargo on Castro era CUba.&nbsp;</li><li><b>Full scale invasions</b> - Afghanistan, Iraq.&nbsp;</li></ul></ul>
What is soft power?&nbsp;	<ul><li><b>Soft power&nbsp;</b>can be defined as the ability to produce a positive sum or win win outcomes. Joseph Nye (2004) called it the '<i>power of attraction',&nbsp;</i>placing emphasis on mutual gains and recognising complex interdependence.&nbsp;</li><li>Soft power seeks to persuade rather than cajole.&nbsp;</li><li>Main foreign policy instruments are - <b>aid, trade, diplomacy, alliances, treaties, agreements, multilateral instituion building.&nbsp;</b></li></ul>
Give some examples of states using their soft power	<ul><li>Examples include creation of the Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action (Iran Nuclear Deal, 2015), Good Friday AGreement (1998), Paris Climate Accord (2015).&nbsp;</li><li>Diplomacy may result in arms reduction treaties such as the Washington Treaty in 1987, which eliminated a whole class of weapons from Europe.&nbsp;</li><li>Diplomacy fostered much greater East-West co-operation. May have been responsible for the peaceful resolution of the cold war.&nbsp;</li><li>According to Softpower30, France is the most adept at using soft power, but the EU is recognised as having a long soft power reach.&nbsp;</li></ul>
What is smart power?&nbsp;	<ul><li>A combination of soft and hard power instruments to achieve one's objectives.&nbsp;<br></li><li>For example, the effective use of superior American military power and attractive political ideology could be seen as 'smart power' and led to them winning the cold war</li><li>Could be soft power backed up by the possible use of hard power.&nbsp;</li></ul>
