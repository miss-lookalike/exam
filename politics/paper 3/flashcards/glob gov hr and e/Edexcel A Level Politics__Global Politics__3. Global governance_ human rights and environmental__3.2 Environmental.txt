Where did the term Tragedy of the Commons originate from?	<ul><li>In the 1830s when Victorian economist William Foster Lloyd argued that common land tended to be overgrazed. Free rider problem.&nbsp;</li><li>The positive benefit that each herder gained by adding one more animal (in meat, wool, leather) exceeded the negative impact on the quality of the pasture, which was small and shared by all herders.&nbsp;</li></ul>
What did Garett Hardin argue in 1968?	<ul><li>He likened the environment to a&nbsp;<b>global commons -&nbsp;</b>Areas and resources that are shared but not regulated. Causes the&nbsp;<b>free rider problem.&nbsp;</b></li><li>He believed that the Earth has a limited carrying capacity for the size of the population. The optimum capacity had been reached and any further increases would lead to a deteroriation in the quality of the environment and of human life. Increased pollution, deforestation, etc.&nbsp;</li><li>To prevent this, Hardin believed that there should be limits on world population growth.&nbsp;</li></ul>
