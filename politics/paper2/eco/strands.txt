Deep Green - Champions ecocentrism and thus rejects the view that humans are more important than other species or the natural world. Key concepts: environmental consciousness, ecocentrism and strong sustainability

Shallow Green - Endorses enlightened anthropocentrism thus accepts the lessons of ecology principally to sustain human life and achieve human objectives. Key concepts: weak sustainability. AKA environmentalism.

Social Ecology - Identifies the root of humans’ oppression of nature to be systems put in place by humans to exploit their fellow humans. This means that environmental degradation can be linked to existing social structures and sustainability is linked to radical social change.

Eco Socialism - The belief that the capitalist system is to blame for societies problems including the degradation of the environment over time

Eco Anarchism - An ideology that believes in the removal of the state and that the state is to blame for the environment’s degradation. Anti productivism and extractivism

Eco Feminism - Calls for an egalitarian collaborative society with no dominant group. Believes nature’s and women’s problems come from men.
