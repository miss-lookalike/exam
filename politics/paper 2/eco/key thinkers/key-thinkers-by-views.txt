### **Rachel Carson**

**Human Nature:**

* Humans tend to see nature as something to be used for their own gain rather than having value in itself.
  

**State:**

* The state must take steps to protect the environment, especially the seas and rivers.
  

**Society:**

* Society must change its attitude toward the natural world radically, to one of protection rather than exploitation.

**Economy:**

* Industry pollutes because it works in the interest of big business. Therefore, industry must be prevented legally.

### **Aldo Leopold**

**Human Nature:**

* We have become separated from the natural world and must re-establish our affinity with it.
  

**State:**

* It is the state's duty to protect the environment.
  

**Society:**

* Urbanisation and industrialisation have removed society’s connection with the natural environment.
  

**Economy:**

* The drive towards industrial growth has destroyed the natural environment.

### **EF Schumacher** *(Buddhist)*

**Human Nature:**

* We have lost our sense of humanity and connection with others and the natural world. Instead we live with the material certainties of living and industry.
  

**State:**

* The overbearing State should be replaced by smaller decentralised institutions.
  

**Society:**

* Small-scale societies can meet human needs without creating artificial wants.
  

**Economy:**

* Self-sufficiency on a small-scale is good; scale should match human needs.
* Work should be rewarding.

### **Murray Bookchin** *(Socialist)*

**Human Nature:**

* We have lost our sense of humanity and social connection.
* Humans have the capacity to live in harmony with each other and the environment.
  

**State:**

* The state is the problem.
* It must be replaced by self-government.
* A democratic and sustainable society must be formed to replace it.
  

**Society:**

* Natural forms of hierarchy must be replaced by new forms of community and equality.
* Society should be remade in the image of ecological balance and mutualism.

**Economy:**

* Modern capitalism incapacitates ecologically sustainable development.
* It must be replaced by a confederation of autonomous eco-communities, linked by an anarchist order.

### **Carolyn Merchant** *(Feminist)*

**Human Nature:**

* We live in a society which is hierarchical, and that affects our relationship with nature.
* We need to nurture nature rather than dominate it.


**State:**

* The state reinforces patriarchy and the domination of nature.
* Needs to be restructured rather than nurture.


**Society:**

* Society must move away from domination of nature and women.
* Environmental awareness must go hand-in-hand with feminist consciousness.


**Economy:**

* Capitalism is exploitative and benefits from both ecological and social destruction.
