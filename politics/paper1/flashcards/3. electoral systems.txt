How does AMS work?	<ul><li>Voters have 2 votes&nbsp;</li><ul><li>One for a constitutency representative, who is elected using FPTP&nbsp;</li><li>Another for party list regional constituency&nbsp;</li></ul><li>Then use party list system to 'top up' members using the d'Hondt formula</li></ul>
What are the advantages of AMS?	<ul><li>The top up component adds a proportional element, corrects FPTP.&nbsp;</li><li>FPTP element maintains a strong link between member and constiency.&nbsp;</li><li>Electors have more choice than under FPTP. Can vote for a 'split ticket', for different parties on list and constituency.&nbsp;</li><li>More than one representative - every party can potentially win a seat and parties cannot just ignore parts of the country</li></ul>
What are the disadvantages of AMS?	<ul><li>Creates two different type of member - one with constiency resonsibility and one without.&nbsp;</li><li>A closed list system is used, perhaps gives too much power to the parties.</li><ul><li>The voter choses, but the party have a hierarchy. But the person does vote for the party anyways. And it might allow parties to field minority candidates. They can put women to the top of their list.&nbsp;</li></ul><li>Still benefits larger parties - not perfectly proportional. The SNP still dominates Scotland and Labour benefits from top up seats in Wales</li></ul>
Where is STV used?	<ul><li>The Northen Irish Assembly&nbsp;</li><li>Scottish Local Elections&nbsp;<br></li></ul>
How does STV work?	<ul><li>Multi member constituencies.&nbsp;</li><li>Voters rank their choices.</li><li>In order to be elected, a candidate needs to achieve a quota, arrived at using the <b>Droop formula</b>,which divides the number of votes cast by the number of seats contested plus one.&nbsp;</li><li>If your candidate doesnt win enough vote, your vote is transferred to another candidate.&nbsp;</li><li>Or if you candidate already has enough votes to win a seat, then your vote is transferred to your second choice.&nbsp;</li></ul>
What are the advantages of STV?	<ul><li>Close correlation between votes and seats&nbsp;</li><li>Plenty of <b>voter choice</b> as it is possible to <b>choose between candidates</b> <b>standing for the same party, as well as between candidates from different parties</b>.&nbsp;Takes power away from parties. <b>Fewer wasted votes</b></li><li>More than one representative - every party can potentially win a seat and parties cannot just ignore parts of the country</li><li>Has created a power sharing government in Northern Ireland that ended 30 years of violent disturbance.&nbsp;</li></ul>
What are the disadvantages of STV?	<ul><li>Not fully proportional, particularly where multi-member constitencies are used</li><li>In larger, multi member constituencies, the link between members and voters may be weak.&nbsp;</li><li>Power sharing agreements are still weak. The Northern Ireland assembly has been suspended since Feb 2022 after Paul Givan resigned in protest of the NI protocol</li><li>Can be <b>confusing for the voter&nbsp;</b>and lead to&nbsp;<b>donkey voting.&nbsp;</b>Many people are unable to distinguish between candidates.&nbsp;</li></ul>
