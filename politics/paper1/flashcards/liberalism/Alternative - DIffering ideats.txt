Social ____ /explain	- Different ideas on how large of a role state should play (mod/clas) - Classical believe state should lay down conditions for an orderly existence, provide defence and avoid interference  - Also support laissez-faire economic system   - Also endorse social darwinism on the basis of natural selection within society -> individuals differ in abilities and it is unavoidable that some will fail whilst others will succeed -> against state welfare
Different views of the state - Modern liberals    Believe in ___ state (explain) Arrived due to what historical background? Argued for what consequently	- Modern liberals believe in an enabling state (state should enable individuals to reach self-fufilment and therefore freedom) - Arrived at this position due to growing awareness of inequality during the 19th century  - Modern liberals of the time saw the appalling working/living condititions and called for state welfare to bring about equality of opportunity  - Argued that if some were disadvantaged due to social circumstances -> state should intervene with aim to correct

Economic policy - Modern liberals	- Keynesian style economic management supported, some state intervention is needed to enable the economy to operate effecienty and to recover from a recession  - Support capitalism as the best economic system
What are the similarities between classical and modern liberals? (4)	- Both seek to enhance individual freedom -> just in different ways -> both believe individuals are rational with inherent value   - Both endorse equality of opportunity and equal civil rights    - Both wish to place limits on power of state eg limited gov etc -> state should be as small as possible but understand it as necessary   - Both support fundamental concepts of private ownership and capitalism
